MOTOR POWER FACTOR CORRECTION & CAPACITOR SIZING
===================================================

Formula:
   Q_c = P_in * (tan(phi_1) - tan(phi_2))
   Q_c = (P_shaft / efficiency) * (tan(acos(PF_1)) - tan(acos(PF_2)))

Capacitance Required per Phase:
   Delta Connection (400V 50Hz):
     C_delta = (Q_c * 10^9) / (3 * 2 * pi * f * V_LL^2)   [microfarads per phase]
   Star Connection:
     C_star = 3 * C_delta
