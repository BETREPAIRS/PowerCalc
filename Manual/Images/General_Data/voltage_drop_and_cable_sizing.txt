VOLTAGE DROP & CABLE AMPACITY (IEC 60364-5-52)
==================================================

1. Voltage Drop Equations:
   Single-Phase:
     Delta_V = (2 * L * I * (R * cos(phi) + X * sin(phi))) / 1000   [Volts]
   Three-Phase:
     Delta_V = (sqrt(3) * L * I * (R * cos(phi) + X * sin(phi))) / 1000   [Volts]

   Percentage Voltage Drop:
     % Delta_V = (Delta_V / V_nominal) * 100%

   Limits: Lighting circuits: <= 3%; Power/motor circuits: <= 5%.
