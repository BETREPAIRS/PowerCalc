VID-01: STAR-DELTA STARTING PROCEDURE & ELECTRICAL INTERLOCKS
===============================================================

Objective: Reduce starting current inrush to 1/3 (approx 33%) of Direct-on-Line value.

Key Components:
- KM1: Main Line Contactor
- KM2: Delta Running Contactor
- KM3: Star Starting Contactor
- KT1: Star-to-Delta Transition Timer (Electronic On-Delay, set to 5-8 seconds)
- F1: Thermal Overload Relay

Sequence of Operation:
1. START Pushbutton depressed: KM1 and KM3 energize simultaneously.
   - Motor windings are connected in STAR (Y).
   - Voltage across each phase = 230V (on 400V supply).
   - Motor accelerates up to ~80% of synchronous RPM.
2. Timer KT1 times out (after 5-8 seconds):
   - Auxiliary NC contact opens -> KM3 (Star) drops out.
   - Guaranteed pause interval (30 ms to 50 ms transition delay).
   - Electrical interlock contact ensures KM3 is fully open before KM2 can energize.
3. KM2 (Delta) energizes:
   - Motor windings are connected in DELTA (Δ).
   - Full 400V applied across each phase winding.
   - Motor runs at full rated torque and speed.
