VID-02: MOTOR INSULATION RESISTANCE (MEGGER) TESTING
======================================================
Standard: IEEE 43 / IEC 60034-27-1
Instrument: 500V DC Insulation Tester (for 400V motors)

Preparation:
1. Lock Out / Tag Out (LOTO) all electrical feeds.
2. Confirm zero voltage using a calibrated multimeter/tester.
3. Disconnect motor leads from the Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) or electronic soft starter to prevent semi-conductor component damage.

Testing Procedure:
1. Phase to Ground:
   - Connect Earth probe (-) to motor frame bare metal / ground terminal.
   - Connect Line probe (+) to U1, then V1, then W1.
   - Apply 500V DC for 60 seconds.
   - Minimum acceptable insulation resistance:
     * New motors: > 100 MΩ
     * Motors in service: >= 5 MΩ
     * Critical danger threshold: < 1 MΩ (windings must be dried/baked or rewound).
2. Phase to Phase:
   - Remove terminal links (U1-V1, V1-W1, W1-U1).
   - Test insulation resistance between separate winding phases.
3. Discharge all residual capacitive charges by grounding winding leads before touching.
