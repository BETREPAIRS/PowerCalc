VID-03: TRANSFORMER INSULATION & POLARIZATION INDEX (PI)
==========================================================
Standard: IEEE C57.12.90 / IEC 60076

Testing Configurations:
1. High Voltage to Low Voltage (HV - LV)
2. High Voltage to Ground (HV - GND)
3. Low Voltage to Ground (LV - GND)

Polarization Index (PI) Calculation:
   PI = R_10min / R_1min
   (Ratio of insulation resistance at 10 minutes to insulation resistance at 1 minute)

Interpretation:
   PI < 1.0 : Dangerous / Failed (contamination, extreme moisture)
   1.0 <= PI < 1.5 : Questionable
   1.5 <= PI < 2.0 : Adequate
   PI >= 2.0 : Good / Healthy dielectric condition
