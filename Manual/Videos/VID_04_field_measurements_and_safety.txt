VID-04: ELECTRICAL FIELD MEASUREMENTS & SAFETY PROTOCOLS
=========================================================

1. Multimeter Safety Categories:
   - CAT IV: Origin of installation (utility service entrance, electricity meters).
   - CAT III: Distribution level (feeders, panelboards, motor control centers).
   - CAT II: Single-phase receptacle-connected appliances.
   Always ensure probes and meters are CAT III 600V or CAT IV 600V rated when working in industrial panels.

2. Three-Point Earth Ground Testing (Fall of Potential - IEEE 81):
   - Current stake C placed at 100% distance D (approx 30m - 50m).
   - Potential stake P placed at 62% of distance D from earth pit.
   - Measure resistance: target < 5 ohms (commercial) / < 1 ohm (substations).

3. Infrared Thermography Criteria:
   - Temperature rise Delta_T <= 10 deg C: Normal operation.
   - 10 deg C < Delta_T <= 30 deg C: Medium defect (schedule maintenance/tightening).
   - Delta_T > 30 deg C: Critical fault (immediate shutdown / risk of arc flash or fire).
